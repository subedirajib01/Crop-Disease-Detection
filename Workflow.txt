Why this structure?
- raw: Original downloaded images. Never modify these.
- processed: Cleaned and resized images.
- split: Images organized for model training and evaluation.
- notebooks: Python experiments and pre processing code.
- models: Saved trained models (Phase 3).


Suppose you currently have PlantVillage and later download two additional datasets.

Organize them like this:
